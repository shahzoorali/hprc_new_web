1. ABOUT THE COMPETITION
The HPRC Equestrian Challenge 2026 is an open equestrian competition organised by the
Hyderabad Polo & Riding Club (HPRC), Gandipet, Moinabad, Ranga Reddy. The competition is open
to all eligible riders from HPRC and affiliated clubs and units and aims to promote equestrian sport in
the region while providing riders a competitive platform across all age categories and ability levels.
This edition comprises the 40 cm Show Jumping class — a dedicated entry-level height catering to
beginner and younger competitors, making the competition accessible to a wider group of riders.

5. HORSES
a. The health, wellbeing and fitness of all horses are paramount to HPRC. All units / clubs are
mandated to ensure that the horses brought for the competition are fit.
b. The Arena Steward will check the horse for its fitness to compete. If a horse is found to be unfit /
lame / has open wounds, it will not be allowed to compete. Additionally, if the judges deem the
horse to be unfit for the competition, they are empowered to disallow the horse from
participating.
c. Riders are responsible for the care and safety of their horses during the event.
d. Number of Entries: A horse can participate only once in each category of an event /
discipline.

8. OTHER INFORMATION
Withdrawal, Change, Transfer or Substitution of Entry:
a. Withdrawal: The Entry fee of a horse entered for any event will not be refunded if the horse does
not participate in the class.
b. Horse-Rider Combination: Once an entry has been submitted for a horse-rider combination, any
change to this will be considered as a new entry with the payment of the entry fees as stipulated.
Cancellation: The Organizing Committee reserves the right to cancel any class which has less than
four entries, or for any other reason at their discretion. In that case, the entry fee will be refunded.
Whips/Spurs/Bits: As per EFI and relevant FEI rules.
Apparel: All riders must comply with EFI Dress Code Regulations during the event. Non-compliance
may result in disqualification.
Appeals / Objection: A written appeal / objection may be lodged two hours before the
commencement of any class and within half an hour of announcement of the results. A fee of ₹2,000
must be deposited with the Show Secretary, along with the appeal / objection. The deposit will be
refunded if the appeal / objection is upheld.
9. ELIMINATION / DISQUALIFICATION
Authority of the Ground Jury: During the event, the Ground Jury holds the authority to disqualify,
eliminate, or evict a Person Responsible (PR) for violations of the FEI Code of Conduct. Such actions
may be taken for:
a. Equine Welfare: Any instance of horse abuse.
b. Conduct Violations: Unsportsmanlike or disrespectful behavior toward officials, staff,
volunteers, or other participants. This standard applies to the PR as well as their entourage
(including family, coaches, grooms, owners, or support staff).
c. Safety: Failing to follow sport-specific regulations or safety requirements, such as mandatory
headgear rules.
d. Ethical Standards: Any actions that contradict the FEI Code of Ethics.

10. MEDICAL & VETERINARY
First aid facilities will be available at the venue. A Veterinary Officer will be available on-call
throughout the competition. Emergency medical services for riders will be on standby. The organisers
will not be responsible for any medical or veterinary expenditure incurred.
Infectious Disease Documentation: Upon arrival at HPRC, all horses must be accompanied by valid
negative test results for Equine Infectious Anemia (Coggins) and Glanders (utilizing CFT or Mallein
methods). To be accepted into the camp, these certificates must have a validity of a minimum of 15
days prior to arrival. Please be advised that entry to the premises will be strictly denied for any horse
lacking the required documentation.
