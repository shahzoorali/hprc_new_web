1. ABOUT THE COMPETITION
The objective of the National Qualifier (NQ) is to enable qualifications to JNEC.
The National Qualifier 2026 will be held from 12th to 14th August 2026 at the Hyderabad Polo &
Riding Club (HPRC), Hyderabad, under the aegis, technical guidelines, rules and regulations of the
Equestrian Federation of India (EFI).
All participants are advised to go through the National Qualifier Guidelines 2026 before the event.

5. HORSES
a. Horses from any grade except for Grade 1 and above in Show Jumping and Grand Prix in
Dressage are eligible for entering NQs.
b. One horse can participate only once in each category of any discipline.
c. In a day, a horse can be used in a total of three (3) events only as follows:
- Two (2) Dressage and One (1) Show Jumping; or
- Two (2) Show Jumping and One (1) Dressage
6. EFI REGISTRATION
a. Horse Registration: All horses participating in EFI-approved events must possess an EFI
Passport. No entry will be accepted without a valid EFI Registration Number.
b. Rider Registration: All riders must be registered with EFI. Entries will not be accepted without a
valid EFI Rider ID.
c. Foreign Nationals and OCI Card Holders cannot participate in the National Qualifier 2026.
7. ENTRIES AND FEES

all requested details and paying the relevant entry fees. Manual entries are not accepted.

a. The Camp will open at noon (12:00 pm) on 12th August and close at noon (12:00 pm) on 14th

b. Stables are limited and will be allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

9. OTHER INFORMATION
Medals: No medals will be awarded in any category in the National Qualifier 2026. Riders qualifying
for further stages will be notified by EFI.
Withdrawal, Change, Transfer or Substitution of Entry:
a. Withdrawal: The Entry fee of a horse entered for any event will not be refunded if the horse does
not participate in the class.
b. Horse-Rider Combination: Once an entry has been submitted for a horse-rider combination, any
change to this will be considered as a new entry with the payment of the entry fees as stipulated.
Whips/Spurs/Bits: As per EFI and relevant FEI rules.
Apparel: All riders must comply with EFI and FEI Dress Code Regulations for that discipline during
the event. Non-compliance will result in disqualification.
Appeals / Objection: A written appeal / objection may be lodged two hours before the
commencement of any class and within half an hour of announcement of the results. A fee of ₹2,000/-
must be deposited with the Show Secretary, along with the appeal / objection. The deposit will be
refunded if the appeal / objection is upheld.

10. MEDICAL & VETERINARY
First aid facilities will be available at the venue. A Veterinary Officer will be available on-call
throughout the competition. Emergency medical services for riders will be on standby. The organisers
will not be responsible for any medical or veterinary expenditure incurred.
Infectious Disease Documentation: Upon arrival at HPRC, all horses must be accompanied by valid
negative test results for Equine Infectious Anemia (Coggins) and Glanders (utilizing CFT or Mallein
methods). To be accepted into the camp, these certificates must have a validity of a minimum of 15
days prior to arrival. Please be advised that entry to the premises will be strictly denied for any horse
lacking the required documentation.
